# Playing as a guest, 10-31

## Try it before you sign up

Back in the BYOND days you clicked a server and you were in. No forms. We want that feeling back: soon you'll be able to
open a world in your browser, type a name, and play as a guest.

Guests get the real game: gather, build, fight what lives in the wild. They choose from Human, Dwarf, Elf or Orc. A
banner tells them the truth from the start: nothing a guest builds is kept.

When a guest leaves, their people don't wander off. Every one of them dies where they stand: half go up in flames, the
rest freeze, crumble to bones or melt away. Even the ones another player captured. Nothing a guest made can be kept by
anyone else.

Unless they sign up. One button, on the banner or in Settings, links their Discord. Everything they built becomes theirs
and saves from that moment, like any other village.

Players can do to a guest anything they could do to anyone. Guests never start a war. They only fight back.

Question for you: did you ever spend a whole night building on a server as a guest, only to lose it all at the end?
