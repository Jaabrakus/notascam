# No first house, 11-01

New villages no longer get a house planned for them. Before this, the game planned your first house or lodge right
after Begin and kept one villager building it until it was done. Playing as a Frogman on the public server, the owner
found that one unit was always tied to the hut and chose "No first house". Your people now start free. You lay out
your own walls in Plan mode, and place beds, doors and workshops from Place.

## Done

- **The first house is off.** After Begin, nothing is planned. No villager is put on the job board, and none is kept
  for building.
- **Villages that already have one keep it.** A first house planned before today stays that realm's own plan. Pause it
  or remove it from the Work tab if you don't want it.
- **It can come back.** It's one switch, so it can be turned on again on a server without changing the game.
