# Current worn-art direction

The user rejected the automatic component-fitting preview as looking squashed and
pasted on. The active sample links now open this complete-character master gallery.
Do not restore the experimental compositor as the main preview.

Deepguard Iron is the approved visual reference. New worn masters are drawn with
the built-in imagegen tool. Artwork and exact prompts are preserved alongside each
other. The current gallery contains the approved reference, sword/shield, leather,
dwarf gold, revised staff idle and revised spear/shield idle.

In staff and spear v2, both side views put the pole forward of the chest. East has
the near right gripping arm; West has the far right gripping arm. The spear v2 has
the shield on the left arm. Earlier v1 drafts remain saved but are not active.

Long poles use padded 128px exports with a 64px body space, to preserve the full tip
and shaft. These are complete standing character masters, not reusable equipment
overlays or an implemented animation system. Sixteen facings are confirmed as the
eventual target for both armor and weapons; the user asked to fix the worn look first.

The other component-design proposals remain in `../racial-redraws-v1/`. Several
were previously cut at incorrect equal-width boundaries. Its builder now detects
transparent gutters per row and invalidates stale fit previews when crops change.
Those corrected source cuts do not by themselves prove a production-quality fit.
