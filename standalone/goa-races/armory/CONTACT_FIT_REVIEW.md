# Equipment contact-sheet fitting review

The first pass renders all **63 drawable designs** alone on **Human, Dwarf and Kobold**, in south/front and east/side views at body frame zero. Each sheet records the selected actual material. A design that executes or changes pixels is not thereby fitted correctly.

The reference render was generated by `fit_review.py`; `fit-review-manifest.json` records the renderer SHA-256 and the included IDs. Contact sheets are candidates for visual inspection, not evidence that every material, body variant, direction and animation frame is approved.

## Concrete findings from the first pass

| Area | Finding | Correction to test |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral side-held long weapons | `weapon.longsword`, arming sword, spear, halberd, harpoon and staff heads overlap the face in east view. Their handles are near the hand, but the blade/pole extends through the face. | Rotate long neutral weapons about the unchanged hand anchor roughly +20–25° in east and the opposite angle in west. Check the whole 16-angle transition and each attack pose. Short daggers, hammers and bows need separate assessment instead of blindly applying the same correction. |
| Native GOA sword | The generic cropped-box pivot makes the blade cross the body high in front and horizontally at the waist in side view. Its source cells are not a complete four-view held-weapon set. | Replace the arbitrary `w*.48,h*.82` crop pivot with measured source-frame hilt registration. Reuse mirrored complete artwork for unsupported directions, explicitly labeled as a fitted reconstruction. See the source evidence below. |
| Boots | `feet.boots` reads as a brown sole underneath visible bare feet on all three tested bodies. | Test raising the anchor from Y59 to Y58 and increasing target height from `.095*bodyHeight` to `.12*bodyHeight`, then verify front/side and every walk frame. Cover the ankle/foot while keeping the sole around the body ground line Y61. |
| Helmets | Kettle, nasal, horned, winged and crested designs retain visible eyes on these three base bodies. Great helm hides the face as the original closed design intends. | Long elf ears, hair and hybrid horns still require their own foreground/behind-helmet masks. These base-body samples do not establish those fits. |
| Chest armor | The ordinary DM chest designs generally register at the torso in these neutral front/side views. `goa.chest` is considerably boxier and broader than the DM plate/mail designs, especially across shoulders. | Inspect GOA body and shoulder layers separately; tune width/height for each short or slender body family rather than resizing their combined bounding box as one object. |
| Leg armor | The original plate/mail/scale layers read as short tassets/skirt/waist armor. They do not cover the whole leg. | Preserve their actual design; do not describe them as full trousers or full limb protection. A new greave/trouser design would need separate art. |
| Shields | Buckler, round, heater, kite and tower show plausible front placement in these base neutral samples. Side views are often mostly hidden by the body because they occupy the far arm. | Check west/north and moving frames; disappearance in east alone does not prove the source is empty or incorrectly attached. |

## GOA sword source evidence

`goa-grip-review.png` displays original 32-pixel weapon, base body and composite frames enlarged with pixel axes. The original `sword1/swordover.dmi` blank movement state has four frames and four declared directions. Pixel inspection establishes:

| Source view | Frame-zero opaque source pixels | Source content |
|---|---:|---|
| South | 49 | Full diagonal blade and dark handle. Handle occupies approximately X8–11, Y20–22. A useful inferred grip pivot is **(10.5,21.5)** in the original 32px frame, or **(21,43)** in 64px fitting space. |
| North | 1 | One white pixel; no measurable handle or complete held blade. The same remains true in all four walking frames. |
| East | 51 | Full horizontal blade and dark handle. Handle X9–11, Y21–22, guard near X12; useful inferred pivot is approximately **(21,43–44)** in 64px space. |
| West | 17 | A blade fragment at X1–9,Y21–22, with no handle. Other walking frames contain fragments of 21–30 pixels. |

These are measured image features and inferred registration points; the source does not provide an authored hand socket. South frames one and three shift their handle up one 32px pixel. East's last frame shifts its handle roughly +5px X and -1px Y compared with the first. Consequently a fixed crop-relative pivot cannot register all native frames.

For the updated three-frame race gait, a robust initial fitted reconstruction can reuse complete south/east neutral artwork, mirror the east frame for west and the south frame for north, and let each body's actual hand anchor supply movement. Use `(21,43)` for the original complete image, `(43,43)` for its horizontally mirrored 64px frame, and draw the far-hand version behind the body. This is a reconstruction for the updated body, not a claim that the original north/west held frames were complete.

## Scope of approval

The review establishes reproducible visual coverage of all designs on three representative neutral body shapes and identifies the specific corrections above. It does **not** certify all 454 material combinations, all 94 registered bodies, walk registration, attacks, physical two-handed grips, missing-limb behavior, or production game integration. Those need their own visual and runtime checks.
