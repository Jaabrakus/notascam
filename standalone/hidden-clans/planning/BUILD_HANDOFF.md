# Building and workshop handoff scope

The October 17 scoped pass covers **Carpentry recipes/destruction; wooden and stone floors/walls; and wooden and stone bridges**. Browsing and cancelling Carpentry menus keeps assigned work running. A real craft attempt (including an original random failure that consumes material), destruction, or successful covered placement pauses the actor's finite queue and other automatic controllers. Task objects, pending order, role configuration and production configuration remain available for explicit Resume.

Carpentry captures the original selection, rechecks ownership/roster/liveness after prompts, and rejects a deleted workshop. It also rejects a change to the actor's captured Race, SubRace or IsMist profile after any prompt, including arrow quantity. Changing selection during a recipe prompt does not redirect the recipe to the new unit or pause that unit. Ingredient/range rejection leaves work alone. Arrow crafting preserves the original RNG-before-quiver order: a no-effect missing/full-quiver result does not pause; actual material consumption or arrow creation does.

Covered terrain placement hands off only inside successful terrain gates. Rejected placements no longer clear the existing destination. Original costs remain intact: wooden/stone floors require the respective carried material without consuming it; walls and bridges consume material. Ground/supported-sky floor and wall gates, race-specific stone art and wall HP, building skill changes, upper-level support creation, and bridge hazard cleanup remain unchanged. Stone bridges retain liquid, chasm and lava branches; wooden bridges retain liquid and chasm branches.
