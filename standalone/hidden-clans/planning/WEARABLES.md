# Wearables: names, recipes and levels

Generated by `art/enhance/catalogue.py` (edit the tables there and run it again); every item is also in `art/enhance/catalogue.json` for wiring into the game. Sprites: `art/enhance/wearables64.py`. Status: proposal, not in the game yet.

## Changes in this revision

- **Gentler levels.** Training slows by half every 10 levels (Skills.dm), so the top tiers sat almost out of reach (hellforged needed 85-95) and new units, who start every skill at 1, could not use iron (Sword 20). Now, to use: wood and leather 1, bone 5, iron 10, chitin 20, silver 30, gold 40, adamantium 50, diamond 60, hellforged 70.
- **Smaller style steps.** Heavy styles add at most +5 (they reached +10); light ones take off 3.
- **RuneScape bar counts.** A sword is 1 bar, a longsword 2, chainmail 3, platelegs 3, a breastplate 5, a kite shield 3, a nasal or kettle helm 1, a great helm 2.
- **Two-handed weapons take both hands.** Battle axes, halberds, pickaxes and staffs leave no hand for a shield; the kits drawn so far are changed to match (the orc and the frogman put their shields away).
- **Weights**, scaled from the game's own (an iron sword 25, a dagger 10): heavier styles and gold weigh more, chitin and adamantium less.
- **School robes.** The game's black, red and white silk robes stay as they are. New robes weave in a stone or relic and give +10% spell power in one school: Astral (diamond), Chaos (toxic gland), Flames (ruby), Priest's (gold), Druid's (emerald), Necromancer's (skull).
- **Wield checks use the Remastered combat levels** (Attack, Defence, Magic; Mining for pickaxes), as RuneScape does. The old magic schools (Astral, Chaos, Destruction, Holy) never train in the game, so they are flavour names only; staffs and enchanted weapons ask for Magic 5-30.
- **Names.** Studded Leather Jerkin, Leather Breeches, Leather Bracer, Skull Helm (bone, made with a skull as the game's bone helmet is), Wooden Club, Skull Mace, "Diamond" in place of "Diamond-set", Banded Mail without the race in the name. Wooden weapons are all wood.

## How it works

- **Made where the game already makes gear.** Forge (Smithing), Leatherworks (Leatherwork), Bone crafting (Bone craft), Carpentry (Woodwork), Gem cutter (Gem cutting). Enchanted weapons are finished on a powered rune circuit (Runes.dm), which pays the enchantment's mana from its crystals.
- **Two levels per item.** *Craft*: the workshop skill needed to make it. *Use*: the Remastered combat level needed to wield or wear it (CombatSkills.dm): Attack for weapons, Defence for armour and shields, Magic for staffs and school robes, Mining for pickaxes; enchanted weapons need Attack and Magic. The weapon still trains its own sub-skill (Sword, Axe...). Levels are the Remastered skill levels (1-99).
- **Quality** still comes from the crafter's skill, as the Forge does it now: Poor <=20, Average <=30, Quality <=40, Impressive <=50, Grand <=60, Masterful <=75, Epic <=90, Legendary 100+ (the Forge's own rule). Damage and defence below are at Quality; better crafters make better copies of the same item.
- **Recipe = the tier's material x the style's count, plus extras** (grips are skin, hafts and shield boards are wood). The original recipes take one material each; these cost more so that heavy gear means mining and smelting.
- **Two hands.** Battle axes, halberds, pickaxes and staffs take both hands: no shield while one is held.
- **Weight** counts against what a unit can carry, as the game's item weights do (an iron sword is 25, as now).
- **Race rules** are the Forge's own: adamantium is dwarf work, goblins and kobolds do not work silver or gold, orcs do not forge straight swords, kobolds do not set diamonds. Banded mail is dwarven. Hell-forged gear backfires on anyone under its level who is not a demon or half-demon (the game's existing rule). Race rules limit who can *make* an item; anyone with the level can use it.

Staffs are made at the Carpentry; their head needs the Gem cutter's cut stone. They are wielded with Magic, not a weapon skill.
