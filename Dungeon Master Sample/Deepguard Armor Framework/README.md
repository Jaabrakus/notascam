Every body has named source, independent armor layers, coverage masks, equipped atlas, four standing direction PNGs, pose attachments and a manifest. Existing motion has an animated preview. The viewer supports filtering, whole-outfit and per-piece offsets for every supplied frame, PNG/recipe export and source/equipped comparisons. East and West default to Y=-2, as requested.

Fits are drafts for review. Shared profiles follow anatomy rather than sex or crafting origin; per-body/per-frame exceptions can be saved. Motion uses supplied body frames and separate head, torso, hand and foot attachments. Missing motion is not synthesized. Female sources currently cover ten races. New attack art, injury-aware gear, hidden surfaces and sixteen facings remain pending.

Registry snapshots and source hashes record the exact inputs. The source registries and artwork are unchanged.
