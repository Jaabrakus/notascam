# Worn Armor and Idle Poses

Current complete-character artwork and sprite exports. The spear/shield and staff folders contain the corrected v2 poses.

Each folder contains the high-resolution artwork, a four-direction sprite sheet, individual South (front), North (back), East (right-facing) and West (left-facing) PNGs, a frame manifest and the generation prompt.

All bodies use 64px fitting space. Spear and staff frames are padded to 128 × 128 to preserve the full pole; their body origin is (32,32). Other frames are 64 × 64. The direction PNGs keep their frame padding and shared registration. These are standing views; motion and sixteen-direction versions are not included.
