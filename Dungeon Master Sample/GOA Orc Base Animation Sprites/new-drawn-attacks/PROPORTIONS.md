# Orc anatomy constraints

The user confirmed that the Orc must keep short, stocky legs matching the supplied
appearance-layer reference. Newly drawn attack poses must not elongate it into a
human athlete. Keep the original broad torso, large head, thick arms and broad feet.
Use knee bends, hip rotation and body lean to make kicks readable; never lengthen
the leg bones to achieve reach. The reference's hybrid colors and appendages do
not belong in the base Orc sheets. Body-only, hard pixels, no smoothing.

The old assembled-part action export is rejected as the final artwork method.
New whole-body frame drawings are required. Generated art is still a draft until
pose consistency and native-scale registration are checked.

## Latest direction and limb constraints

Keep anatomical left/right attacks distinct in all four facings. In front view, character right is screen left; in back view character right is screen right. East right limbs are near, West right limbs are far. Preserve the non-striking hand in guard and the non-striking foot as the support. Back-facing punches travel away into depth, never as a sideways arm extension.

User approved the front-facing kick motion in references/user-approved-front-kick.png: guard, knee chamber, foreshortened sole toward camera, recovery. Keep this front kick as the motion reference while drawing the right-side counterparts.

## Hand detail preference

Use small, simple mitten-like ends of arms. Avoid large open palms, individual fingers, claw silhouettes and unnecessary hand detail. Use hand detail sparingly only when needed for a readable action. Preserve existing approved sprites unless a specific revision is requested.
